Roborock’s IFA 2026 lineup spanned three distinct environments: pool, home and garden. The company presented the RockAqua P1, Saros 20 Flow Complete and RockNeo Q2 LiDAR. Roborock’s [IFA announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/roborock-redefines-human-centered-cleaning-at-ifa-2026-unveiling-a-comprehensive-ecosystem-from-the-living-room-to-the-pool-302869541.html) and [Engadget’s event coverage](https://www.engadget.com/2250013/roborock-shows-off-a-series-of-new-vacuums-at-ifa-2026/) provide details on the lineup.

## Pool: RockAqua P1

Roborock’s first pool-cleaner line is designed to tackle the floor, walls and waterline. Automatic waterline parking is a practical ownership detail: retrieval should be as straightforward as deployment.

## Home: Saros 20 Flow Complete

The roller-mop system and threshold-crossing chassis address two everyday challenges — cleaning the floor and moving between rooms. The relevant question is how the robot handles the home, not simply another suction figure.

## Garden: RockNeo Q2 LiDAR

Roborock rates the mower for lawns up to 800 m². It uses LiDAR and vision-based navigation without boundary wires or an external antenna, making simpler installation part of the proposition alongside the mowing itself.
