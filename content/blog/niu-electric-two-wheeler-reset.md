NIU just broke 1.12 million sales in 2025, making them the second-largest pure electric two-wheeler brand in the world.

And the 2026 lineup they unveiled at EICMA is not incremental. It is a reset.

## The NQiX 1000: Performance Redefined

The headline model hits 125 km/h with triple removable batteries and over 100 km of real-world range. This is not concept-stage thinking. It is a production-ready machine competing head-to-head with combustion scooters on the metrics that actually matter: speed, range, and daily usability.

Built-in Google Maps navigation across the full NQiX series means riders do not need phone mounts or workarounds. It is integrated, not bolted on.

## FQiX: Smart Urban Riding

The FQiX brings rear radar, NFC unlocking, and a 5-inch TFT display, starting at EUR 2,399. For a connected, safety-aware urban scooter at that price point, there is not much competition.

Rear radar is a feature typically seen on premium bicycles and motorcycles. Putting it on an electric scooter signals where NIU sees this category heading: toward genuine transportation, not just last-mile convenience.

## XQi 500: Off-Road Goes Electric

The XQi 500 is a 28.8 kW electric dirt bike at just 92 kg. Street-legal. This is NIU stepping completely outside their comfort zone, and doing it with specs that demand attention.

## From City Commuter to Full Portfolio

What most people do not realize is how fast NIU has moved from city commuter scooters to a full electric mobility portfolio covering urban, performance, and off-road segments. All connected, all smart, all built for European roads.

The pattern is clear: start with one category, prove the technology, then expand outward. NIU went from a single Kickstarter scooter in 2015 to 1.12 million units a decade later. The 2026 lineup is what happens when a brand decides its original category is too small.

The demand for premium electric two-wheelers in Europe is real. Riders just need brands that take design, range, and connectivity seriously. NIU does.
