Another summer, another wave of wildfire-smoke headlines — and air purifiers selling out again. What used to be a nice-to-have is quietly becoming household infrastructure.

## Built for the Bedroom

That is why Levoit's newest launch caught our attention. The Eleva 300X isn't pitched as a gadget for allergy season — it's designed for the bedroom. A quick-response 3-channel AirSight Plus 2.0 sensor tracks PM1.0, PM2.5 and PM10 in real time, fully sealed 3-stage HEPA filtration captures 99.97% of airborne particles like pollen and dust, and Auto Mode adjusts fan speed on its own. In Sleep Mode it runs at around 20 dB — quiet enough that it doesn't wake you up.

## Moments, Not Specs

That's the pattern we keep seeing with Levoit: they don't chase specs, they chase moments in daily life. First clean air for pet owners, now clean air for sleep. Category by category, they're turning air quality into something people can actually feel.
