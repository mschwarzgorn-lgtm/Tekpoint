The robotic lawn mower market is entering a new phase of maturity — and Segway Navimow is leading the charge with an industry first.

## First-Ever TÜV Rheinland Lawn Care Certification

Navimow's X420 and i206 AWD models have earned the first-ever TÜV Rheinland "Lawn Care" certification. The testing protocol simulated a full year of high-frequency mowing under European conditions, evaluating the impact on lawn health over time.

The result: minimal lawn impact at Level 2 on TÜV's 5-level scale, meaning virtually invisible traces even after extended use. No other robotic mower brand has achieved this certification.

## The Technology Behind It

At the core of this achievement is Navimow's Xero-Turn™ AWD steering system. Unlike conventional robotic mowers that damage turf during turns, the Xero-Turn system eliminates this problem entirely. The X420 handles slopes up to 30° without losing traction or compromising lawn integrity.

This is not a marketing claim — it is independently verified by one of Europe's most respected testing and certification bodies.

## What This Means for the Category

The robotic outdoor category has evolved from a niche gadget segment to a mainstream home automation essential. Navimow is pushing the category forward with engineering that prioritises lawn health alongside cutting performance.

As European homeowners increasingly adopt smart outdoor solutions, certifications like this set a new quality benchmark for the entire industry.
