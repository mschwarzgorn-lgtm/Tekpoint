At IFA Berlin 2026, Tekpoint and AGIBOT signed a strategic master distribution agreement.

For Tekpoint, this is an important step in building our robotics business: combining AGIBOT’s technology with the market access, logistics, integration and local support that customers need to put it to work.

## A Portfolio for Different Applications

The portfolio on our [robotics page](/en/robotics/) includes the A2 Ultra, X2 Ultra and G2. These platforms open up different conversations — from customer interaction and events to manufacturing, automation and research. The right model and configuration depend on the application, not just the demonstration.

## Distribution Beyond Moving Hardware

That is where distribution has to go beyond moving hardware. It means working with resellers, system integrators and customers to evaluate the use case, coordinate supply and provide a local point of contact, including support and returns coordination.

## Building Useful Applications

For Tekpoint, the opportunity is not simply to bring more robots to market. It is to help turn promising technology into useful applications, with a commercial structure behind it that customers can rely on.

Thanks to the AGIBOT team and our Tekpoint colleagues for bringing this partnership to the next stage. Signing at IFA was an important milestone. Now the focus is on building the business together.

*Tekpoint is AGIBOT’s regional distributor for Europe. [Explore Tekpoint Robotics](/en/robotics/) and discuss your project with our Robotics team.*
