The European e-scooter market is projected to reach $4.7 billion by 2033, and every year demand concentrates in the summer months. The Xiaomi Electric Scooter 6 series arrives ahead of that peak as the strongest micro-mobility lineup the company has brought to Europe.

## A Deliberate Approach to Timing

Rather than rushing early-season availability, Xiaomi focused this spring on refining the Scooter 6 range, building inventory, and stabilising supply. The result is a launch positioned to meet demand precisely when it is highest, rather than ahead of it. In micro-mobility, aligning inventory strength with peak-season demand is a decisive advantage.

## Five Models, Every Segment Covered

The Electric Scooter 6 series spans five models, from the entry-level Lite to the flagship Ultra. The Ultra features a 1,200W peak motor, 12-inch tires, dual suspension, and an iF Design Award. This breadth allows the lineup to serve value-focused commuters, performance-oriented city riders, and everyone in between within a single, coherent range.

## Why Preparation Defines the Season

Summer is when scooter sales concentrate, and success depends on readiness rather than being first to market. Tekpoint has prepared accordingly, with stocked warehouses, secured logistics, and distribution ready across the DACH region. Peak season does not reward the first mover — it rewards the best prepared.
