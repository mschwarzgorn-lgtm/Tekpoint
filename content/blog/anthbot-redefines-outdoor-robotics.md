## Three years from zero to global

Three years. That's all it took for ANTHBOT to go from startup to a global operation across 30+ countries — and they've just planted their European flag in Neuss, Germany.

What caught our attention isn't the speed of their expansion. It's the intelligence behind their product.

## The 4-in-1 revolution

Most robotic mowers do one thing: cut grass. ANTHBOT's N Series is a modular 4-in-1 system that mows, mulches, collects clippings, and sweeps fallen leaves — autonomously handling the cleanup that every other robot leaves for you. Their Cyclone System captures 99% of clippings into a 23-litre bag, delivering a carpet-like finish without human intervention.

## Autonomous vehicle tech in your garden

The navigation stack tells the real story: Full-Band RTK positioning, 360° LiDAR, dual-vision cameras, and AI obstacle detection for over 1,000 object types. This is autonomous vehicle-grade technology, miniaturised for your garden.

## CES 2026: a debut to remember

At CES 2026, ANTHBOT debuted with over 10 awards — including Best in Show from The Ambient and an Innovation Award from Trusted Reviews. The industry noticed. So did we at Tekpoint.

## The bigger picture

Outdoor robotics is following the exact trajectory we saw with indoor cleaning robots five years ago. The brands that solve the full problem — not just the obvious part — are the ones that win. ANTHBOT gets this.
