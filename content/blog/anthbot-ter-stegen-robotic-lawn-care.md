A world-class goalkeeper promoting robot lawn mowers. If that doesn't tell you where this industry is heading, nothing will.

ANTHBOT has announced Marc ter Stegen as their Global Brand Ambassador — a move that underscores how far robotic lawn care has come in just a few years.

## The ANTHBOT Lineup

ANTHBOT's product range now spans entry-level to professional-grade outdoor robotics:

- **Genie 3000** — The flagship that put ANTHBOT on the map. Handles lawns up to 3,600m² with Full-Band RTK, AI-powered vision, and 4G connectivity.
- **M Series (M5 & M9)** — Compact, wire-free models designed for urban gardens. The M5 starts at an accessible price point, while the M9 covers lawns up to 1,000m² with RTK and dual vision AI.
- **N8** — The standout from CES 2026. The world's first 4-in-1 robot mower that mows, mulches, collects clippings with 99% efficiency, and sweeps fallen leaves. One machine replacing four tasks is not incremental improvement — it is a category shift.

## From Niche to Infrastructure

Three years ago, robotic lawn care was a niche hobby for tech enthusiasts. Today, ANTHBOT is winning CES awards, earning features on publications like 9to5Mac, and signing world-class athletes as ambassadors. The category has matured from "interesting gadget" to genuine home infrastructure.

Robotic lawn care just grew up.
