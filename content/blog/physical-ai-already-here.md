Forrester's Top 10 Emerging Technologies for 2026 confirms what the industry has been building toward: AI is no longer confined to software. It's moving into the physical world — powering robots, vehicles, and ambient experiences that change how people live.

## The Shift That Matters

The report places humanoid robots as a medium-term technology — 2 to 5 years to meaningful impact — while agentic commerce is already delivering ROI for early adopters. But the shift that matters most for consumers? AI is now embedded in the devices you interact with every day.

## Already in European Homes

Look at the products reshaping European households right now:

- **Roborock's StarSight** builds real-time 3D models of your home for precise navigation
- **Navimow's Xero-Turn** navigates complex gardens autonomously with centimetre-level accuracy
- **Xiaomi's HyperOS** connects entire households through a unified AI platform
- **Renpho** pairs physical wellness devices with digital mindfulness applications

Physical AI isn't a future prediction — it's already on European shelves, in European homes, and in European gardens.
