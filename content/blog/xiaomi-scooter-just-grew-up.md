The Xiaomi Electric Scooter 6 lineup represents a serious generational leap in micro-mobility — one that closes the gap between recreational gadget and genuine commuter vehicle.

## Performance That Matters

The Scooter 6 series delivers specifications that put it squarely in commuter territory:

- **1200W peak motor** with 2.4-second acceleration response
- **75km range** on a single charge
- **12-inch all-terrain tires** with dual-swing-arm suspension
- **IPX6 waterproofing** for all-weather riding
- **TCS anti-slip system** — traction control technology adapted from the automotive industry

## Award-Winning Design

The entire Scooter 6 lineup swept the prestigious iF Design Awards, recognising both the aesthetic and functional design achievements of the series. These are not just high-performing vehicles — they set a new standard for what electric scooters look and feel like.

Micro-mobility is no longer a future trend. It is the present — and it is accelerating.
