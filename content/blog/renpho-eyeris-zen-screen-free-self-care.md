# A screen-free pause

After a week focused on fashion and beauty, there is room for a different kind of personal-care story: a routine that is not about changing how we look.

RENPHO x Headspace Eyeris Zen combines adjustable heat and compression with nine built-in Headspace guided meditation tracks. The audio is built into the eye massager, supporting a screen-free wind-down routine. This is self-care technology, not a cosmetic treatment or a promise to cure sleep problems.
