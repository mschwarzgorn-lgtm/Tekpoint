# CARE PRO — A unique technology coming from Japan to Europe

## Looking ahead to 2027

Following Paris Fashion Week’s womenswear Spring/Summer 2027 calendar, the beauty conversation also invites a longer-term look at technology. CARE PRO DEEP is a Japanese hair-care device whose European arrival is expected in 2027. This is a preview of technology to watch for next year, not an announcement of current European availability.

## A specific role in a care routine

CARE PRO DEEP is an at-home ultrasonic hair-treatment infuser. The brand describes ultrasound designed to help an applied, compatible penetration-type hair treatment enter the hair. The device works alongside the treatment; it is not the treatment formula itself, and it is not a straightener.

## Preparing for a clear introduction

For retailers and distributors, meaningful innovation needs a clear explanation. Compatible products, instructions and the intended use of the device will matter as the European introduction approaches. No specific launch quarter or country-by-country availability is announced here.
