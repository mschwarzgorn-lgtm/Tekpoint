Tekpoint has become a partner and co-owner of Harmonix, alongside taking on distribution for the brand across its European markets. It is a deeper commitment than a standard distribution agreement, and it reflects a clear conviction about where wearable audio is heading.

## An Icelandic Approach to Technology

Harmonix is an Icelandic company, and that origin matters. Iceland builds technology that has to simply work in demanding conditions: practical, resilient, and honest. That philosophy runs through the company's products and its roadmap.

Rather than competing in the noise-cancelling race to shut the world out, Harmonix is doing the opposite. It is building a continuous hearing ecosystem designed to keep people connected to the world around them.

## A Tremendous Opportunity in Smart Glasses

Smart glasses represent a category where practical, price-sensitive solutions remain badly underserved. Harmonix's SonaVue addresses exactly that gap. SonaVue is eyewear that combines open-ear audio with real-time AI translation across more than 159 languages, in frames designed to be worn all day.

There is no earbud to insert and no screen to stare at. SonaVue quietly carries calls, music, and conversations across languages. This is where wearable AI becomes genuinely useful: not another device demanding attention, but a layer that makes the real world easier to navigate.

## A Category Nobody Has Truly Cracked

The larger opportunity lies in hearing enhancement, a category that remains underserved despite enormous demand. Millions of people do not have problems serious enough to justify a medical device, yet there has been no great over-the-counter solution built for them.

Harmonix's Elevate line is built precisely for that gap. Designed to look like premium earbuds rather than clinical hardware, Elevate brings hearing enhancement into everyday consumer technology. On average, people wait between seven and ten years before seeking help with their hearing, held back by cost, clinical friction, and stigma. The shift to over-the-counter solutions changes that equation, turning a slow medical purchase into accessible consumer technology.

## Why This Partnership

Combining practical innovation, real style, and a sharp focus on underserved customers creates the conditions for a brand that can stand out in a crowded market. Innovation, design, customer focus, and a genuine impact on everyday lives are what define Harmonix, and they are what made this partnership the right move for Tekpoint.

Tekpoint will bring Harmonix to its European markets with the same value-added distribution model it applies across its portfolio: market entry, EU compliance, logistics, and retail connectivity. As a co-owner, Tekpoint is invested in the long-term success of the brand, not just its shelf presence.
